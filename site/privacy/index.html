Privacy

This page covers authorkit.pro and resources.authorkit.pro. Last updated 1 October 2026.

Neither site asks for your name or email. There are no accounts, and neither site sets cookies.

Two tools on resources.authorkit.pro save what you enter in your own browser, so it is still there when you come back: the text you paste into a book design template, and the ticks on the KDP paperback preflight checklist. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's data for the site removes it.

Both sites are hosted by Cloudflare and use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not follow you to other sites. Cloudflare handles this under its own privacy policy.

The book design template pages load their preview fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you open one of them. Google's privacy policy applies to that connection. Everywhere else, fonts come from our own servers.

Links to other sites, such as KDP or Instagram, take you to pages with their own privacy policies.
