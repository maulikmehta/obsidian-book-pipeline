"Curiouser and curiouser!" cried Alice.

She was now more than nine feet high, and she at once took up the little
golden key and hurried off to the garden door.

Poor Alice! It was as much as she could do, lying down on one side, to look
through into the garden with one eye; but to get through was more hopeless
than ever, and she sat down and began to cry again.
