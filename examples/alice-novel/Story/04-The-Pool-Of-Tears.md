"Curiouser and curiouser!" cried Alice (she was so much surprised, that for
the moment she quite forgot how to speak good English).

"Now I'm opening out like the largest telescope that ever was! Good-bye,
feet!" (for when she looked down at her feet, they seemed to be almost out of
sight, they were getting so far off).

She was now more than nine feet high, and she at once took up the little
golden key and hurried off to the garden door.

Poor Alice! It was as much as she could do, lying down on one side, to look
through into the garden with one eye; but to get through was more hopeless
than ever: she sat down and began to cry again.

"You ought to be ashamed of yourself," said Alice, "a great girl like you,"
(she might well say this), "to go on crying in this way! Stop this moment, I
tell you!" But she went on all the same, shedding gallons of tears, until
there was a large pool all round her, about four inches deep and reaching
half down the hall.
